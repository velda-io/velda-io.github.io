  <div class="space-y-6">
    <div class="rounded-xl border border-[#d8d8d8] bg-white p-6">
      <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mb-2 text-[#222]">How much does GPU compute cost on Velda?</h3>
      <p class="text-color-[#575858]">Velda offers transparent pay-as-you-go pricing. H100 GPUs start at $3.30/hr (spot) or $4.90/hr (on-demand). H200 GPUs start at $3.80/hr (spot) or $5.90/hr (on-demand). All prices include GPU, CPU, memory, and ephemeral storage with no hidden fees. For B300 Blackwell GPUs, <a href="/reserve" class="text-[#0b63f6] hover:underline">contact us for reservation pricing</a>.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rounded-xl border border-[#d8d8d8] bg-white p-6">
      <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mb-2 text-[#222]">What is included in the GPU pricing?</h3>
      <p class="text-color-[#575858]">All GPU pricing on Velda is all-inclusive. Each GPU instance includes the GPU itself, CPU cores, RAM, ephemeral disk storage, and networking. There are no separate charges for CPU, memory, or storage. Learn more about our <a href="/serverless-gpu" class="text-[#0b63f6] hover:underline">serverless GPU infrastructure</a>.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rounded-xl border border-[#d8d8d8] bg-white p-6">
      <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mb-2 text-[#222]">Can I reserve GPU capacity in advance?</h3>
      <p class="text-color-[#575858]">Yes, Velda offers GPU capacity reservations for B300, H200, and H100 GPUs with flexible terms from 1 month to 5 years. Reserved capacity provides guaranteed availability and cost savings. You also receive spot credits for unused reserved capacity. <a href="/reserve" class="text-[#0b63f6] hover:underline">Reserve GPU capacity now</a> or <a href="/b300-gpu-reservation" class="text-[#0b63f6] hover:underline">learn about B300 reservations</a>.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rounded-xl border border-[#d8d8d8] bg-white p-6">
      <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mb-2 text-[#222]">What is spot pricing vs on-demand pricing?</h3>
      <p class="text-color-[#575858]">Spot pricing offers lower rates (up to 35% savings) for interruptible GPU instances. On-demand pricing provides guaranteed availability at standard rates. Spot instances are perfect for fault-tolerant workloads like ML training, while on-demand is best for production workloads requiring guaranteed uptime.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rounded-xl border border-[#d8d8d8] bg-white p-6">

No credit card required to start. For enterprise needs or B300 reservations, <a href="/reserve" class="text-[#0b63f6] hover:underline">reserve capacity</a> or <a href="/contact" class="text-[#0b63f6] hover:underline">contact our team</a>.</p>
